About Us

Built Around Brands. Driven by Possibilities.

For more than two decades, Glam has been working at the intersection of brands, businesses and markets.

What We Believe

We believe great brands should not stand still. They should evolve, enter new categories, reach new consumers and create new opportunities—without losing the equity that made them iconic.

(01) Our Story

From Licensing to Brand Growth.

Glam began with a simple belief: every great brand has untapped potential.

Over the years, our work has evolved from traditional licensing into a broader brand and business consulting platform.

Today, we work with global brands, entrepreneurs, manufacturers and businesses to identify opportunities, develop partnerships and build commercially sustainable brand programmes.

Our experience spans technology, consumer electronics, appliances, fashion, lifestyle, FMCG, F&B, automotive, mobility and education.
