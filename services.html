Brand Strategy & Consulting

A strong brand needs a strong strategy. We help organisations understand where their brand can go next—and how to get there.

03

Market Entry & Expansion

Entering a new market requires more than ambition. It requires local knowledge, relationships and the right execution partner. Glam helps international brands identify opportunities, assess markets and establish strategic partnerships for market entry and expansion.

04

Brand Extension

The strongest brands have the ability to live beyond their original category. We identify relevant opportunities to extend brand equity into new categories, products, channels, consumer segments and experiences.

05

Partner Identification & Development

The right partner can determine the success of a brand programme. We identify and evaluate potential partners based on capability, distribution, market reach, brand fit, investment, manufacturing and growth potential.

Our objective is not simply to find a licensee. It is to find the right long-term partner.

06

Product & Category Development

We help partners translate brand identity into commercially relevant products. From concept development and category selection to positioning and go-to-market strategy, we bridge the gap between brand promise and product opportunity.

07

Retail & E-Commerce

Modern brands need to win wherever consumers discover and purchase products. Our expertise extends across modern trade, e-commerce, marketplaces, retail networks, D2C and distribution.

08

International Business Development

We connect brands, businesses and opportunities across markets. Our global network enables us to identify potential collaborations and commercial opportunities that may not be visible through traditional channels.
